ONE TOKEN MODEL

The network does not need five competing tokens.

TRACE, VECTOR, NEXUS, FORGE and VAULT are designed as parts of one network. Giving every module its own token would split liquidity, governance attention, community energy and market understanding.

The proposed model uses one X100 supply as the common economic reference point. Products can generate usage and revenue independently while the treasury can coordinate how capital is retained, redeployed or used under published policy.

FAQ

The model in plain language.

What is X100?

X100 is the proposed single network token connecting the economic model around MEMEFLOW-related intelligence, trading, automation, launch and treasury modules.

Why use one token instead of a token for every product?

The goal is to avoid splitting liquidity, attention and economic coordination across multiple independent assets while still allowing each product to have its own function.

What is the proposed supply?

The current draft uses a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 X100, with 60% allocated to public / circulation and the remaining 40% distributed across ecosystem, VAULT, team, liquidity and builders / partners.

What is VAULT?

VAULT is the Working name for the network treasury layer. Its role is to hold designated reserves and route approved capital to operations, ecosystem development, liquidity or other published treasury actions.

Does buyback / burn mean holders are guaranteed a return?

No. The draft buyback / burn mechanism is a possible treasury policy. It is not a promise of returns, yield, price appreciation or a guarantee that purchases will occur.

Will TRACE, VECTOR, NEXUS or FORGE have separate tokens?

Not under this model. They are Working names for network modules designed to coordinate around X100 rather than introduce separate product tokens.

Can these parameters change?

Yes. The page describes a draft economic model. Supply mechanics, allocations, vesting and treasury policies can be revised before final launch terms are adopted.
