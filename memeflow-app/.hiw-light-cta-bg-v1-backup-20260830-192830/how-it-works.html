FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

The short version, without blockchain jargon.

Is a Vault PDA another normal wallet?

No. A PDA is a Solana program-derived account. It does not work like a normal wallet with its own seed phrase. It is controlled through the Smart Vault Program.

Does MEMEFLOW get my connected wallet private key?

No. Your connected-wallet private key stays in your wallet. MEMEFLOW can request a signature, but it does not need you to upload that private key.

Why move trading funds into a Vault PDA?

It creates a clear boundary between your normal wallet and the balance allocated to automation, while allowing the on-chain program to enforce the permissions of that automated balance.

Can the executor ignore my rules?

The executor is designed to submit instructions through the Smart Vault Program. The program is the enforcement layer: an instruction that does not satisfy the permitted path should be rejected.

Do users pay the Smart Vault deployment cost every time?

No. The program is platform infrastructure and is deployed separately. A user may still incur normal Solana transaction fees or account-rent costs when their own on-chain accounts are created or used.

Does this remove trading risk?

No. The architecture controls permissions and execution paths; it cannot remove market volatility, token risk, slippage or losses from trading.
